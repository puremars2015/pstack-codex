# In your own words

Play back the desired outcome and the problem it solves in plain language. Include relevant constraints and what would count as done. Label assumptions and meaningful ambiguity. Do not add requirements or start implementing unless the same request asks you to proceed. Ask at most the clarification that materially changes the work.
