# Why it exists

State the decision or behavior whose rationale needs evidence. Inspect the relevant code, git history, blame, commits, PR discussion, and referenced issues. Discover connected documentation or issue tools only as useful; absence of a connector is not evidence that no decision exists. Use only sources accessible under the user's scope. Do not message authors to ask.

Build a timeline separating contemporary statements, later interpretation, and your own inference. Look for disconfirming evidence and superseding decisions. Explain the original constraint, alternatives considered, and whether that constraint still applies. Cite the records directly. If the rationale is undocumented, say so and distinguish it from your best explanation.
